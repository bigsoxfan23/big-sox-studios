## The idea

The Multi-AI Council is an experiment in getting useful disagreement from several AI providers without turning the result into a wall of competing answers.

It started with a practical question: if different models have different strengths and perspectives, can they deliberate in a repeatable way and produce one useful recommendation?

## How it works

A question is prepared with the relevant context and fresh research, then multiple providers examine the same decision. Their responses are normalized into a common structure before a final synthesis turns the discussion into something actionable.

The first serious use case is fantasy football, where trades and roster decisions benefit from different analytical perspectives. The underlying council is intentionally reusable, though, so the same pattern can eventually help with purchases, travel, research, and other decisions.

## Design principle

The interesting part is not asking more AIs. It is creating a process where disagreement, evidence, and synthesis actually improve the decision.
